## 9. Quality / AQL benchmarks

Upgraded from `[ESTIMATE]` — AQL 2.5/4.0 is the universal apparel industry standard (ISO 2859-1 Sampling Procedures for Inspection by Attributes), used by virtually all Indian apparel exporters per AEPC and major buying offices.

| Metric | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Major defect AQL (industry standard for menswear & most apparel) | **2.5** | ISO 2859-1; HQTS, AQIS, Apex Fashion Lab, Apparel.wiki — global apparel inspection standard |
| Minor defect AQL | **4.0** | Same — global apparel standard |
| Critical defect AQL | 0 or 1.0 | Same |
| Standard inspection level (bulk garment) | Level II (Normal Inspection) | ISO 2859-1 |
| Typical final-inspection defect rate (passing lots) | 1.5-3.5% `[ESTIMATE]` | Industry norm |
| Defect rate triggering vendor review | >5% `[ESTIMATE]` | Industry norm |
| First-pass-yield target | 90-95% `[ESTIMATE]` | Industry norm |

**Implication:** Major defects (wrong measurements, broken seams, missing components) drive returns; minor defects (light staining, thread pulls, slight shade variation within tolerance) don't. Your model should weight major-defect rate higher than minor in vendor risk scoring.

## How these inform the synthetic data design (Phase 2)

The synthetic PO dataset will use the following distributions:

1. **Vendor pool:** 12 simulated vendors, top-3 concentration at ~60% (§8)
2. **Geographic destination:** ~34% Tier-1, ~36% Tier-2, ~30% Tier-3 (matching Unicommerce 66% non-metro)
3. **Fabric mix:** 55% cotton-based, 30% blends, 15% synthetic (§3 fabric pattern)
4. **Garment type mix:** 75% knits, 25% wovens (Snitch is knit-heavy menswear)
5. **Seasonal demand:** Festive period (Sep-Nov) at 3x baseline volume (§2)
6. **Planned vs actual lead times:** centered on §7 estimates × seasonal amplifiers as noise
7. **Delay label:** order is "delayed" if actual_delivery > planned_lead_time + 3 day buffer
8. **Return rate calibration:** base 22% for fashion D2C (§3); COD multiplier 1.5x; tier-3 multiplier 1.3x; festive multiplier 1.8x
9. **Payment mode mix:** ~60% COD, ~40% prepaid (D2C menswear norm for value tier)
