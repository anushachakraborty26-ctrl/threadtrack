ThreadTrack v2 — data-quality stress test
==============================================================

Corruption: ~30% of categorical values mistyped or blanked, plus missing numerics and qty typos.

Rule scorer (static, hand-built):
  AUC on CLEAN data:  0.801
  AUC on MESSY data:  0.709   drop +0.092

Logistic regression (the MLOps-loop argument):
  trained on CLEAN, tested on CLEAN:  0.861
  trained on CLEAN, tested on MESSY:  0.786   drop +0.075
  RETRAINED on MESSY, tested on MESSY: 0.799   recovery +0.013

Interpretation:
  - A hand-built scorer cannot adapt; it silently rots under drift.
  - ML trained on clean data degrades when reality is messy.
  - ML retrained on the messy distribution recovers part of the gap.
  - This is the argument for a retraining loop, not a frozen v1 model.
